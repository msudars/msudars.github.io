Battlas

A tool for operating telemetry from electric vehicles and battery energy storage systems (BESS). It turns the data a battery produces in normal use into health estimates with stated uncertainty, so an engineer can decide which cells, modules or systems to look at first. It is developed at IFE within a three-year Research Council of Norway project (FME BATTERY) and builds on cellpy for lab data.

What it is trying to solve

Operators of EVs and battery storage already collect voltage, current, temperature and time. What they usually lack is a defensible answer, per unit, to three questions: which unit is off, how fast is it degrading, and how sure are we? Capacity tests need the asset taken out of service, and most estimators return a single number with no statement of when it can be trusted.

  • Evidence, not just an estimate. Every output carries an uncertainty interval, and where a unit's operating conditions fall outside what the data can support, the tool says so and abstains instead of guessing.
  • Several measures of health, not one score. Exposure, capacity trend, resistance and deviation from sibling units are reported separately, so you can see why a unit moved.
  • Lab to field as a prior, not a copy. Models trained on lab cycling lose accuracy when the duty changes, so lab data sets the starting expectation and each unit's own field data updates it.
  • Cells sit inside modules and systems. Field labels usually exist only at module or string level, so the model treats cells as parts of a hierarchy, not as independent rows.

It runs on CPU, on your own machine, so telemetry never has to leave site. The first step with a new dataset is a screening pass on your own export. Try the interactive demo of the "I don't know" idea. In active development: write to me if you have telemetry to try it on.

Research themes

My PhD thesis at Purdue: Low-Data Machine Learning Framework for Generalizable Battery Degradation and Fault Diagnosis (2026).

Degradation prediction from limited data

Forecasting long-term capacity fade from a small amount of cycling data, with models light enough for a battery management system. DegradAI, npj Clean Energy 2025.

Health monitoring in operation

Detecting the onset of rapid degradation while the battery is in use, for electric propulsion. Batteries, 2024.

Fault diagnosis across fleets

A fault-diagnosis framework that adapts to unseen EV fleets from different manufacturers using self-supervised pre-training and few-shot fine-tuning.

Safety and abuse

Early detection of thermal runaway, internal temperature measurement and nail puncture. Scientific Reports, 2023 and Energy, 2023.
