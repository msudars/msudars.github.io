What I'm working on

Open source · batteries · at IFE

Battlas

A tool for operating telemetry from electric vehicles and battery energy storage systems (BESS). It turns the data a battery produces in normal use into health estimates with stated uncertainty, so an engineer can decide which cells, modules or systems to look at first.

It works on partial cycles and mixed duty, with no need for full charge–discharge cycles, and builds on cellpy for lab data.

In active development. If you have telemetry you would like to try it on, or want to follow the work, write to me.

What Battlas is trying to solve

Operators of EVs and battery storage already collect telemetry: voltage, current, temperature and time. What they usually lack is a defensible answer, per unit, to three questions: which unit is off, how fast is it degrading, and how sure are we? Standard capacity tests need the asset taken out of service, and most estimators return a single number with no statement of when it can be trusted.

Battlas is built around four ideas:

  • Evidence, not just an estimate. Every output carries an uncertainty interval, and where a unit's operating conditions fall outside what the data can support, the tool says so and abstains instead of guessing.
  • Several measures of health, not one score. Exposure, capacity trend, resistance, and deviation from sibling units are reported separately, so you can see why a unit moved.
  • Lab to field as a prior, not a copy. Models trained on lab cycling lose accuracy when the duty changes, so lab data sets the starting expectation and each unit's own field data updates it.
  • Cells sit inside modules and systems. Field labels usually exist only at module or string level, so the model treats cells as parts of a hierarchy, not as independent rows.

It runs on CPU, on your own machine, so telemetry never has to leave site. The first step with a new dataset is a screening pass on your own export.

Know-it-all Friend

Research themes

Degradation prediction from limited data

Predicting long-term capacity fade from a small amount of cycling data, with models light enough to run on a battery management system. See: DegradAI; autoencoder network for onboard BMS degradation prediction (Journal of Energy Storage, 2024).

Health monitoring during operation

Detecting the onset of rapid degradation while the battery is in use, for electric propulsion. See: In operando health monitoring using deep learning (Batteries, 2024).

Safety and abuse

Early detection of thermal runaway, internal temperature measurement and the effect of nail puncture on cell behaviour. See: internal temperatures of 18650 cells (Scientific Reports, 2023); capacity after nail puncture (Energy, 2023).
