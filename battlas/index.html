A battery model that says "I don't know"

Battlas estimates battery health from telemetry and reports how sure it is. This toy shows the core idea. Move the sliders to change how a battery is used. Inside the range the model has seen, you get a tight estimate. Drift outside it and the interval widens, then the tool refuses to give a number.

How is the battery used?

What Battlas says

This is a toy. The numbers come from a made-up formula, not from a real battery or from the Battlas models. The "lab data covers" box is 15 to 35 °C, 0.5 to 2 C, up to 1,000 cycles, invented for the demo. In the real tool, the interval comes from conformal prediction and the support check comes from the data itself.

What it is for

Most health estimators return a number every time, however unusual the battery's duty, and an engineer can't tell a number the model knows from one it is guessing. Battlas gives the interval, says when it is extrapolating, and declines to answer when it has no basis. That way the decision (look at this unit, retest it, or leave it) rests on what the data supports.
